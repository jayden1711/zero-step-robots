This is a hand-designed linear state-feedback controller, `u = 0.1x + 5θ + 0.3ẋ + 0.6θ̇`, embedded in the network. I couldn't run or test it, and the gains come from a rough estimate of the cart-pole dynamics, so treat them as untested. The sign convention assumes a positive pole angle leans toward +x, so pushing the cart that way corrects it.

One hidden unit stays in tanh's near-linear range because its input weights are scaled by 0.1, and the output weight of 10 undoes that scaling. The other 15 units have zero weights and contribute nothing.
